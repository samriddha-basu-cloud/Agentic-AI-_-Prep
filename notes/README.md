# Agentic AI Applications in Operations & SCM — study notes index
Course **Agentic AI in Ops & SCM** (TM2335), **MBA (OM)**, semester-end preparation. Built from Samba's uploads (Oct 2026):
- the **20 session decks** (Sessions 1–20, including the Chain Reaction round briefs and the season debrief);
- the **syllabus** (9 units);
- the two **past papers**: **Trishul Consumer Products** and **Kaveri Motors / Chain Reaction**.

**What the faculty said about the exam 📘:**
- It is case-based applications plus direct numerical problems, with **no standalone theory**.
- The numericals are straightforward, with no extra formulas.
- **Basic concepts of SCM** are required.
- Expect a comprehensive application case covering all concepts, frameworks, projects, class tests, case studies and **the Chain Reaction simulation**, with **particular emphasis on the strategies, decisions and management approaches used in Chain Reaction**.

**Source key used in every file:**
- 📘 = taken from your session decks, syllabus or past papers (exam-safe wording).
- 📗 = standard SCM and AI knowledge added to give complete answers.
- **Exam markers:** ⭐⭐⭐ = very likely to carry marks; ⭐⭐ = likely; ⭐ = good to know.
